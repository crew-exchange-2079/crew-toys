The same thing in a car

    Why do I have to shift at all?

    One gear cannot do everything. A tall gear turns the wheels lots of times for every turn of the engine, so you go fast — but there is very little left to push with. A short gear pushes hard and the engine races to the top of its range.

    The engine is happiest between about 1,500 and 3,500 rpm. That stretch is the power band, and shifting is just how you keep the engine inside it while the road speed changes underneath you.

    The scale above runs from 0 to 6,500 rpm. Drive the car and watch the numbers move: each mark turns green when that gear would keep the engine inside the happy band. Pick the green one.
